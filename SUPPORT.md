# Support

BugBrain is a research toy with serious receipts, maintained on a best-effort basis.

- Questions and reproducibility problems: open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/Veridical-dev/bugbrain/discussions).
- Confirmed bugs and feature requests: open an [issue](https://github.com/Veridical-dev/bugbrain/issues).
- Security or privacy concerns: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Commercial or research collaboration: `contact@veridical.dev`.

Please include your operating system, Python version, BugBrain commit, command, and smallest reproducible input. Do not attach private source code or credentials.
