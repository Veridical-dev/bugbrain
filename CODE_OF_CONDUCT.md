# Code of conduct

BugBrain should be welcoming to people of every background, experience level, identity, and opinion about whether insects should approve pull requests.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Credit prior work and disclose relevant limitations or conflicts.
- Respect privacy and do not publish secrets, private code, or personal information in issues or experiment artifacts.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, and knowingly misleading research claims are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `contact@veridical.dev`. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with fairness to everyone involved.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project. It is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
