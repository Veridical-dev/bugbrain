# BugBrain brand

## Logo

The primary mark is [`assets/bugbrain-logo.png`](assets/bugbrain-logo.png): a friendly fruit-fly head whose compound eyes become graph circuits, with one cyan review check.

Use it with generous clear space. Do not recolor individual graph nodes, add text inside the mark, or imply affiliation with the FlyWire Consortium.

Palette:

- Charcoal: `#121A1E`
- Amber: `#FFB51B`
- Electric cyan: `#16D9E8`
- Warm white: `#F7F4EF`

## Voice

Curious, technically serious, and willing to laugh at the premise. The joke is never a substitute for a control group.

Preferred one-liners:

- “Peer review, but the peer has 139,255 neurons.”
- “The world's first code reviewer powered by a brain too small to understand Jira.”
- “The fly found two bugs. The controls found our humility.”
